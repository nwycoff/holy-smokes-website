About your account and privacy

Signing in connects to a separate account provider for your password and recovery. A code from your budtender links your account to your store record. We keep that link and secure sessions; we don’t save your patient ID or purchase history in this app.

When you order ahead, your order goes to the shop’s point-of-sale system with the name, birth date and customer type already on your store record, plus the medical license number you enter. The app keeps only your order number and status.

Signing up does not subscribe you to marketing. Notifications and personalized offers are not enabled in this first version.

To remove your sign-in account, call the shop. Removing your rewards connection below does not delete your in-store customer record.
